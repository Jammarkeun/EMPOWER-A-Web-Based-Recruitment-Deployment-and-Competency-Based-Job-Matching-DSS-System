# EMPOWER — Implementation Timeline

**Prepared for:** CDE Manpower Services, Sta. Cruz, Laguna
**System:** EMPOWER — Web-Based Recruitment, Deployment, and Competency-Based
Job Matching Decision Support System
**Date prepared:** 10 August 2026
**Target completion:** 20 December 2026

---

## 1. Purpose of this document

This sets out when each part of the EMPOWER system will be delivered, and what
CDE Manpower Services needs to provide along the way.

The second part matters as much as the first. Several stages cannot proceed
without input from your office — sample documents, staff availability for
testing, and a scheduled training session. Where that is the case, it is stated
explicitly with the lead time required.

---

## 2. Where the project stands today

Development began well before this schedule and a substantial portion of the
system is already built and running.

**Working now:**

- **The complete records database.** Applicants, employees, client companies and
  their departments, manpower requests, requirements, deployments, disciplinary
  records, resignations, terminations, and archives. Hosted securely in the
  cloud.
- **The full applicant lifecycle.** An applicant can be registered, screened,
  have documents verified, be evaluated, deployed, managed as an employee, and
  eventually resigned or terminated — with every step recorded and dated.
- **The digital folder system.** Your Folder 1 / 2 / 3 filing is reproduced
  digitally. The system works out which folder an applicant belongs in based on
  which documents have actually been verified, and moves them automatically. No
  manual re-filing.
- **The competency matching engine.** This is the core of the system. It ranks
  applicants against a specific manpower request and explains every point
  awarded, in plain language. It recommends; your HR officer decides.
- **Secure document storage.** Birth certificates, clearances, and medical
  results are stored privately and can only be opened through a temporary link
  that expires after a few minutes.
- **User access control.** Administrator and HR roles with different
  permissions, plus a complete activity log recording who did what and when.
- **The management dashboard.** Live counts, charts, and a "needs attention"
  panel showing outstanding requirements, overdue requests, and expired
  documents.

**Still to be built** — this is what the schedule below covers:

- Printable and exportable reports (PDF and Excel)
- The applicant and employee self-service portal
- Administrator screens for managing user accounts
- Automatic reading of uploaded documents to reduce typing
- Testing with your staff, deployment, and training

## 4. Phase detail

### Phase 1 — Core completion
**10 August – 6 September (4 weeks)**

Completing the remaining features of the main system.

- Reports that can be printed or exported to PDF and Excel: applicant lists,
  employee lists, deployment records, disciplinary summaries, and per-client
  reports
- Administrator screens for creating and managing user accounts
- The applicant and employee portal, where an applicant can check their own
  application status and an employee can view their assignment and file a
  resignation
- In-app notifications for incomplete requirements and deployment readiness

**From CDE:** nothing required.

---

### Phase 2 — Document reading and data preparation
**7 September – 27 September (3 weeks)**

Two things happen here.

First, the system is taught to read uploaded documents automatically, so that
when an applicant's resume or ID is uploaded, the details fill themselves in
rather than being typed by hand.

Second, we prepare to bring your existing records into the system.

> **Needed from CDE by 14 September:**
>
> 1. **Sample documents** — around 10–15 examples of each type you collect
>    (resumes, IDs, clearances, medical results). Photocopies or photographs are
>    fine. These are used only for testing the reading accuracy and will not be
>    kept.
> 2. **A copy of your current records** — whatever Excel files, Google Sheets, or
>    Google Forms responses you presently use for applicants and employees.
>
> Both are needed early. If they arrive late, this phase moves and everything
> after it moves with it.

### Phase 3 — Internal testing
**28 September – 11 October (2 weeks)**

The whole system is tested thoroughly before your staff ever see it. Faults are
found and corrected here rather than during your testing sessions, so that your
staff's time is spent on judgement — does this match how we actually work? —
rather than reporting obvious problems.

**From CDE:** nothing required.

---

### Phase 4 — Walkthrough and first testing round
**12 October – 1 November (3 weeks)**

The first time your office uses the system.

- **Session 1 (week of 12 Oct):** Full demonstration. We walk through the system
  end to end so your staff understand what it does before being asked to test it.
- **Sessions 2 and 3 (weeks of 19 and 26 Oct):** Your HR staff use the system
  themselves with realistic (not live) data, working through the tasks they do
  daily. Every observation is written down.

> **Needed from CDE:**
>
> - **Three sessions of roughly two hours each.** Preferably the same one or two
>   HR staff throughout, since consistency matters more than numbers.
> - **Dates agreed in advance.** Please confirm these when accepting this
>   timeline rather than closer to October.

This phase is where the system stops being what we assumed you need and starts
being what you actually need. It is worth protecting the time.

## 6. What is not included

To avoid any misunderstanding later:

- **Payroll is not part of this system.** Salary computation, payslips,
  government contribution remittances, and 13th month pay are outside its scope
  and were excluded by agreement at the outset. The system holds employment
  records, not financial ones.
- **Biometric hardware is not supplied.** The system stores a biometrics number
  against each employee, but does not connect to a fingerprint device.
- **Attendance tracking is limited to summary information.** Daily time-in and
  time-out capture is not included.

## 7. Assumptions and risks

Stated openly so there are no surprises.

**1. The document-reading feature is the most likely thing to be delayed.**
It relies on a separate piece of specialist software and is the most technically
uncertain part of the project. If it proves troublesome, it will be set aside
rather than allowed to delay everything else. The system works fully without it
— documents would simply be typed in as they are now. We would tell you early
rather than late if this happens.

**2. Testing dates drive everything after them.**
Phases 5 through 8 follow directly from Phase 4. If the October testing sessions
slip by two weeks, the go-live date slips with them, and the academic deadline
in December does not move. This is the single largest risk to the schedule and
it is largely in your office's hands.

**3. The system is hosted on a free service tier during development.**
It pauses automatically after about a week without use. Before any demonstration
or meeting, it needs to be opened once beforehand to wake it — otherwise it will
appear to be down. This is easily managed but worth knowing.

**4. Real applicant data carries legal obligations.**
Once your office begins entering live applicant records, the Data Privacy Act of
2012 (RA 10173) applies to your handling of them. The system is built to support
this — private document storage, access control, and a full activity log — but
the obligations attach to CDE Manpower Services as the organisation holding the
data. We will cover this during training.

**5. Development time is roughly 10–15 hours per week.**
This is a capstone project carried alongside other academic subjects, and the
schedule is built on that basis rather than on full-time work. It includes some
allowance for the unexpected, but not an unlimited amount.
