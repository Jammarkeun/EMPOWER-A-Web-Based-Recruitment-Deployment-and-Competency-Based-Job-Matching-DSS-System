# Email extract — what CDE needs to provide

A shortened version of the timeline, suitable for pasting into an email body when
the full document is attached. Replace the bracketed parts before sending.

---

Subject: **EMPOWER System — Implementation Timeline and Schedule Confirmation**

---

Dear [Ms./Mr. Surname],

Thank you for asking about the time frame for the EMPOWER system. I have attached
the full implementation schedule, and have summarised the key points below.

**Target completion: 20 December 2026.**

A large part of the system is already built and running — the applicant and
employee records, the complete recruitment process from application through to
deployment and separation, the digital version of your Folder 1 / 2 / 3 filing
system, secure document storage, and the competency matching that ranks
applicants against a manpower request and explains its reasoning.

The remaining work runs in eight stages:

| Stage | Dates |
| --- | --- |
| Completing the remaining features | 10 Aug – 6 Sep |
| Automatic document reading, data preparation | 7 Sep – 27 Sep |
| Internal testing | 28 Sep – 11 Oct |
| Demonstration and testing with your staff | 12 Oct – 1 Nov |
| Adjustments from your feedback | 2 Nov – 15 Nov |
| Final testing and acceptance | 16 Nov – 22 Nov |
| Go-live and staff training | 23 Nov – 29 Nov |
| Academic documentation | 30 Nov – 20 Dec |

**There are a few points where I will need help from your office:**

1. **Three testing sessions of about two hours each, between 12 October and
   1 November.** This is where your HR staff use the system themselves and tell
   me where it does not match how you actually work. It is the most valuable
   stage of the whole project.

2. **One training session in the week of 23 November**, for the staff who will
   use the system daily.

3. **By 14 September:** around 10–15 sample documents of each type you collect
   (resumes, IDs, clearances, medical results), and a copy of your current Excel
   or Google Sheets records. The samples are used only for testing and will not
   be retained.

**Could I ask that we agree the October testing dates and the November training
date now**, rather than closer to the time? The rest of the schedule can absorb
small changes, but those dates determine everything that follows, and the
academic deadline in December cannot move.

For completeness, I should restate that payroll is not part of this system, as
agreed at the outset — it holds employment records rather than financial ones.

I am happy to walk through the schedule in person if that would be easier.

Thank you again for your time and support on this project.

## Notes before sending

- Attach `implementation-timeline.md`, converted to PDF. Sending a `.md` file to a
  business contact is a small unforced error — most will not be able to open it.
- Fill in every bracketed field. An unfilled placeholder reaching a client reads
  as carelessness.
- Have your adviser read it first if the timeline forms part of a formal
  agreement with the agency.
- Send it early in the week. A schedule-confirmation request landing on a Friday
  afternoon typically waits until the following week for a reply.
